# Partner Vendor Pipeline Campaign Intake

## Who's your ideal client? What's their industry and revenue level?

Our ideal clients are B2B AI, enterprise software, cybersecurity, data, automation, cloud, consulting, and technology vendors who sell to senior executives.

They are usually doing roughly `$1M-$50M+` in annual revenue, with the strongest fit being companies that already have a valuable solution, a founder/executive-led sales motion, and a need to build more trusted executive relationships.

They are not looking for basic event sponsorship. They want access to qualified executive conversations, long-term relationship building, market intelligence, and pipeline with decision-makers.

## What do your ideal clients want?

They want trusted access to executive buyers and influencers without being treated like another vendor chasing badge scans.

If they could click their fingers, they would want a repeatable way to build meaningful relationships with qualified executives who are actively thinking about AI, transformation, risk, operations, data, automation, and strategic technology adoption.

More specifically, they want:

- More executive conversations
- More qualified pipeline
- More trust before the sales conversation
- More visibility with the right market
- More strategic partner credibility
- Better insight into what executives actually care about
- More reasons to stay in front of prospects over time
- A relationship channel that compounds instead of a one-time event spend

## Why did those solutions not work?

Most event sponsorships are transactional. Vendors pay for visibility, but they do not get trust, context, or durable relationships.

Booths create foot traffic, but not enough qualified executive conversations. Badge scans produce names, not intent. Logo packages create awareness, but they rarely create pipeline. Cold outbound often reaches people before there is trust or relevance. Webinars attract mixed-quality audiences and rarely create deep relationship momentum. Ads create clicks, but the vendor still has to convert cold attention into belief.

The core problem is that these channels usually start with vendor promotion instead of executive contribution.

The buyer feels sold to before they feel understood.

## How does your solution differ from what they've tried in the past?

Mojo does not sell generic sponsorship packages.

No booths, badge scans, or generic logo packages. Partners enter through executive contribution and strategic fit.

Instead of putting vendors on the outside of an event trying to capture attention, we bring the right partners into an executive intelligence environment where they can contribute insight, participate in relevant conversations, and build credibility over time.

The goal is not just exposure. The goal is trusted proximity.

Partners are positioned around contribution, strategic relevance, and relationship-building. Their participation is tracked across events, contacts, publications, sponsorship contributions, and executive engagement history.

## Why does your solution deliver the result your ideal clients want?

Our unique mechanism is the **Executive Contribution-to-Pipeline System**.

Traditional sponsorship asks vendors to buy attention. Mojo requires partners to earn trust through useful executive contribution.

That changes the entire dynamic.

Executives are more open to partners when the partner is not just selling, pitching, or collecting badge scans, but helping shape the conversation around AI adoption, business strategy, risk, transformation, and competitive intelligence.

The mechanism works because it combines:

- Curated executive environments
- Strategic partner fit
- Contribution-based access
- Relationship intelligence
- Event-level engagement tracking
- Company-based partner profiles
- Long-term visibility across events and publications
- Follow-up context based on shared attendance and shared conversations

This gives vendors a better path to pipeline because the relationship starts with relevance and trust, not interruption.
