# Mojo AI Summits LinkedIn Thesis Image Series

Date: 2026-08-21

## Purpose

Create a repeatable LinkedIn image series for Mojo AI Summits that publishes executive AI thought leadership with a subtle event tie-in.

The working format is an editorial thesis image: one provocative executive AI claim, one cinematic business scene, Mojo as publisher, and a restrained event connection.

## Campaign Position

Mojo AI Summits should sound like an executive room forming around a real decision, not a webinar asking for attention.

Use this pattern:

1. Contemporary AI issue
2. Executive consequence
3. Editorial point of view
4. Subtle Mojo event tie-in
5. Conversation CTA

Core line:

> Where executive leaders shape the future of AI.

Preferred CTA:

> Request your executive invitation.

Conversation CTA for provocative posts:

> Do you agree? Disagree? Is this real?

Avoid:

- "Free webinar"
- Generic AI hype language
- Vendor theater
- Claims about attendee outcomes we cannot prove yet
- Naming a guest, company, title, or likeness unless publication consent is confirmed
- Conference promo creative
- Event flyer composition
- Sponsor-style panels or giant CTA bars

## Editorial Art Direction Lock

Think editorial thought-leadership artwork with Mojo as the publisher, not conference promo creative.

Reference energy:

- Fast Company cover
- Wired feature opener
- Bloomberg Businessweek cover

The event tie-in should be subtle enough that the image could live in a business publication without looking sponsored.

Locked rules:

- Small exact Mojo lockup in the upper-left.
- Use approved Mojo logo artwork only.
- Keep AI uppercase in all applications.
- Preserve clear space around the logo.
- One cinematic executive scene.
- One strong thought-leadership line.
- Lots of negative space.
- Cyan used sparingly.
- Cleaner, cinematic, editorial, premium, and restrained.
- No headshots until actual guest photos and permissions are available.
- No icon rows.
- No fake dashboards.
- No dashboard soup.
- No boxed sections.
- No giant CTA bar.
- No fake speakers.
- No generic AI shield, calendar, people, or wingding visuals.
- No extra gradients or effects beyond the official logo artwork.
- No em dashes ever.

## Visual System

Use the Mojo AI Summits brand guide as the source of truth:

- Deep Navy: `#0A0F1E`
- Electric Blue: `#1666FF`
- Cyan: `#00E6FF`
- Slate: `#1B2333`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Format:

- LinkedIn square: 1080 x 1080
- Optional portrait: 1080 x 1350
- High-contrast deep navy environment
- Electric blue and cyan used with restraint
- One cinematic executive scene or metaphor
- No guest portraits until actual guest photos and permissions are available
- Large editorial headline with 5 to 9 words
- Subtle event tie-in, preferably in small footer type or caption only
- Small exact Mojo lockup top left, never stretched or recolored

Reference style translation:

- Keep the punchy thought-leadership claim.
- Replace shock-collage energy with editorial business intelligence.
- Use fewer colors than the reference image.
- Make the event signal quiet enough that the image still feels publishable in a premium business magazine.

## Pilot Concepts

### 1. AI Executive Readiness

Headline:

> AI READINESS IS NOT A VIBE

Subhead:

> Governance, ownership, data, risk, and value have to show up in the same room.

Visual metaphor:

A quiet executive boardroom before a high-stakes decision. One leader studies a single luminous AI readiness brief on a table. The room is mostly negative space, with subtle cyan light and no interface clutter.

Contributor line for caption only:

> Executive input provided by [Approved Names].

Event rail:

> Mojo AI Summits | AI Executive Readiness | Friday, September 4, 2026

Caption opener:

> Most companies do not have an AI problem. They have an ownership problem that AI is exposing.

### 2. AI Data Readiness and Knowledge Strategy

Headline:

> YOUR AI KNOWS WHAT YOU CAN PROVE

Subhead:

> Retrieval, permissions, lineage, and knowledge quality decide whether AI becomes useful or noisy.

Visual metaphor:

A restrained editorial scene of a leader facing an archive wall where only a few verified knowledge paths are illuminated. No database icons, no fake charts, no busy screens.

Contributor line for caption only:

> Executive input provided by [Approved Names].

Caption opener:

> AI does not magically create institutional memory. It reveals whether the memory is usable.

### 3. AI Use Cases That Survive Finance

Headline:

> THE PILOT IS ASKING FOR A BUDGET

Subhead:

> Experiments only survive when finance can see ownership, measurement, and scale discipline.

Visual metaphor:

A single AI initiative document on a dark executive desk beside a marked-up budget page. The composition should feel like a magazine cover, not a software dashboard.

Caption opener:

> The next AI filter is not technical excitement. It is whether the work deserves funding.

### 4. AI Agents, Automation, and Human Handoffs

Headline:

> AI FAILS AT THE HANDOFF

Subhead:

> Agent autonomy needs escalation paths, approval trails, and clearly scoped authority.

Visual metaphor:

An executive corridor or operations room where a handoff is implied through posture, light, and tension, not literal icons or interface widgets.

Caption opener:

> The agent demo is the easy part. The hard part is deciding where autonomy ends.

### 5. AI Security, Governance, and Trust

Headline:

> YOUR AGENTS NEED A CHAIN OF COMMAND

Subhead:

> AI risk moves faster when agents can act, write, trigger, send, approve, or change systems.

Visual metaphor:

A spare executive security review scene with one restricted access threshold and a single AI system presence implied through light. No shields, locks, icons, or dashboard panels.

Caption opener:

> The question is no longer "Can the agent do it?" It is "Who is accountable when it does?"

### 6. AI Budgeting and Investment Priorities

Headline:

> EVERY AI IDEA GETS A PRICE TAG

Subhead:

> 2027 budgets will separate AI theater from operating advantage.

Visual metaphor:

A leadership table with a small set of AI investment dossiers. One dossier is illuminated, the rest recede into shadow. No portfolio wall, no lanes, no charts.

Caption opener:

> AI strategy gets real when someone has to choose what not to fund.

## Guest And Compliance Checklist

Before publishing any image:

- Confirm guest has opted into publication use of name.
- Confirm company use permission before naming employer.
- Confirm title use permission or use a safer role label.
- Confirm portrait/likeness permission and source image quality.
- Confirm the event title and date match the current virtual-event registry.
- Do not include private invite codes.
- Do not include unsupported claims about attendance, revenue, or outcomes.
