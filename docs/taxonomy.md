# MOJO AI Summits Taxonomy

Version: 0.1.0
Last updated: 2026-08-10

This is the canonical language taxonomy for MOJO AI Summits. Use it for website copy, CRM labels, registration flows, marketing collateral, briefs, partner materials, internal checklists, and support scripts.

The goal is to make the network feel curated, executive, and trust-centered without using generic event-industry language.

## Core Positioning

### MOJO AI Summits

An executive connection and intelligence network that uses immersive experiences to accelerate decisions, partnerships, and innovation.

### Executive Intelligence Network

The preferred high-level category for MOJO AI Summits.

Use when describing the full system of councils, summits, briefs, membership, and partner access.

### Executive Intelligence Brief

The canonical term for the published intelligence product created from councils and summit conversations.

Do not call this a whitepaper, insight report, recap, or post-event report in public copy.

### Executive Research Council

The canonical term for virtual executive research sessions.

Councils capture real-world research from executives and professional contributors to build quarterly Executive Intelligence Briefs.

### The Summit

The canonical term for the in-person executive gathering.

Use `The Summit` for the page/navigation concept and `Q1 2027 Dallas Summit` or similar when a specific summit needs date/location context.

## People And Roles

### Featured Guest

An invited person who contributes expertise, frames a discussion, leads a segment, or helps shape the room.

Use for speakers, discussion leaders, high-value contributors, and named experts when they are invited for their contribution rather than admitted through a paid seat.

### Invited Guest

A person personally invited to attend because they fit the cohort.

Use when the invitation is direct and not tied to a partner package or approved paid attendance.

### Independent Professional Guest

A person outside the original invite list who is approved to attend through application or ticket purchase, is professionally qualified for the room, and is not attending as part of a partner package.

Use this instead of `ticket buyer`, `paid attendee`, `external attendee`, `delegate`, or generic `attendee`.

Recommended public language:

> MOJO rooms include featured guests, invited guests, partners, and a limited number of independent professional guests. Every seat is reviewed for fit before it is confirmed.

Recommended policy language:

> A limited number of independent professional guest seats may be approved by application or ticket purchase. Purchase does not guarantee admission until the seat is confirmed.

### Member

A person with a continuing relationship to the MOJO AI Summits network.

Use only when the person has a defined network role beyond a single attendance instance, such as contributor, track chair, advisory board member, alumni fellow, or other approved membership category.

### Contributor

A person who provides substantive, attributable input to a council, summit discussion, track, or Executive Intelligence Brief.

Use when the person's value is the knowledge they bring to the room.

### Track Chair

A member who owns or guides one of the six discussion tracks for a defined period.

Use only when that responsibility has been confirmed.

### Cohort Advisory Board Member

A governance or standards role for the network.

Use for people helping shape the cohort, standards, format, or future direction.

### Alumni Fellow

A continuing relationship granted to a person after their active role in the network.

This follows the person, not their job title or employer.

### Partner

A practitioner company or commercial organization granted access under partner rules.

Use this instead of sponsor or vendor in public-facing strategic copy unless a legal, contractual, or operational context requires a different term.

### Practitioner Partner

A more specific term for a partner who contributes subject-matter substance, operational expertise, or working-session value.

Use when distinguishing high-fit partners from generic event sponsors.

### Sponsor

Use sparingly. Prefer `partner` in public copy.

Acceptable in internal finance, contract, inventory, fulfillment, and legacy setup contexts where the commercial relationship must be explicit.

### Vendor

Avoid for partner-facing and executive-facing copy.

Acceptable only for operational suppliers such as venue, AV, catering, production, software, and logistics providers.

## Seat And Access Terms

### Seat

The preferred object term for access to a room.

Use `seat` instead of ticket when copy needs to preserve the curated, fit-reviewed nature of attendance.

### Independent Professional Guest Seat

The paid or application-approved access category for a qualified external professional who was not on the original invite list.

Use for pricing, application, confirmation, and registration flows.

### Featured Guest Seat

An invited contribution-based seat.

### Invited Guest Seat

A directly invited non-partner seat.

### Partner Seat

A seat allocated through a partner package, subject to partner participation rules.

### Ticket

Avoid in premium public copy.

Acceptable in payment, receipt, legal, finance, or platform contexts where the transactional object must be clear.

## Rules

- Do not imply that the full room is already committed before it is confirmed.
- `150` is a cap, not an attendance claim.
- `Six` is a format, not a participation claim.
- `Four` is a cadence, not proof of completed rooms.
- Every public access term should preserve fit review and curation.
- Do not describe MOJO as selling access to executives. Describe partner access as participation under room rules.
- Avoid language that makes executive guests feel like leads, prospects, or inventory.
