## Scope of Work

Your responsibilities will include, but are not limited to, the following:

### VIP Program and Advisory Support

- Direct the VIP program for attendees with speaking roles or roundtable leadership roles.
- Schedule VIP briefings for speakers and roundtable leaders as needed.
- Support planning for the VIP dinner and advisory or VIP group expectations.
- Help capture board and VIP recommendations and convert them into event actions.
- Support VIP role lists, VIP trackers, VIP reminder sequences, and VIP scheduling tools.
- Support the advisory board lead with selected executive, advisory board, VIP group, speaker, and moderator follow-up.

### Agenda, Session, and Programming

- Help define event tracks and the six-track boardroom programming model.
- Own the boardroom block timing model, currently planned as a 15-minute presentation followed by a 15-minute roundtable.
- Build the hourly boardroom cadence and room rotation model for the six-hour event.
- Help define the call for papers or speaker nomination process.
- Review speaker submissions and nominations.
- Confirm the speaker lineup with support from Scott and Ron.
- Build session abstracts and public agenda copy.
- Build moderator assignments.
- Confirm which sessions are sponsor-led, sponsor-supported, editorial, private, or Chatham House-style.
- Build and maintain the event run of show.
- Freeze the public agenda unless an approved exception is made.

### Speaker and Moderator Operations

- Build the speaker prep process and schedule.
- Conduct speaker prep calls.
- Collect final slides and presentation materials.
- Support speaker agreements, releases, headshots, bios, and session descriptions.
- Brief moderators and speakers before sessions.
- Handle speaker no-shows, timing changes, concerns, and session readiness issues.
- Send speaker thank-you notes after the event.
- Compile speaker and moderator feedback after the event.
- Update speaker and programming templates based on lessons learned.

### Virtual Webinars, Courses, and Events

- Serve as Program Director for Mojo AI Summits webinars, virtual courses, and other virtual event programming.
- Help define each virtual event or course topic, educational arc, executive decision framing, and intended audience outcome.
- Coordinate featured speakers, moderators, instructors, and optional partner participants for virtual programming.
- Build the run of show for virtual sessions, including welcome, concise briefing, facilitated exchange, close, and follow-up handoff.
- Prepare speaker, moderator, and instructor briefing notes, discussion prompts, sponsor boundary notes, and presentation materials.
- Support registration paths, invite copy, reminder sequences, calendar holds, attendee follow-up, member nominations, and next-event invitations.
- Coordinate technical rehearsal, platform readiness, backup meeting room process, and day-of virtual session flow with the operating team.
- Help make sure recordings, transcripts, chat, attendee data, and content permissions are captured and handled under approved rules.
- Help use webinars and virtual courses to test topic demand, identify speaker and sponsor fit, build executive trust, and warm qualified attendees for Dallas and future city events.

### Event-Day Program Execution

- Confirm schedule readiness.
- Confirm room readiness.
- Confirm executive arrivals with support from The Event Lounge.
- Keep the agenda running on time.
- Manage stage transitions.
- Confirm sponsor sessions or activations begin on time.
- Support room energy, timing, format adjustments, and attendee engagement when needed.
- Support editorial and trust boundaries during sponsored sessions.
- Support capture of notable ideas, conflicts, insights, attendee questions, objections, and recurring themes.

### Trackers, Communications, and Materials

- Set up and maintain the speaker tracker.
- Set up and maintain the attendee tracker for the executive attendee population.
- Set up and maintain the VIP role tracker for speakers and roundtable leaders.
- Build the speaker logistics email sequence.
- Support executive attendee and VIP reminder sequences.
- Prepare speaker green room supplies.
- Support signage, badges, lanyards, sponsor materials, stage assets, printed programs, staff scripts, talking points, and FAQs where Angel is listed as supporting owner.
- Collect session notes, recordings, transcripts, slides, photos, and workshop outputs after the event.

### Cross-Functional Support

You will also support the broader team on venue fit, production needs, floor plans, registration configuration, attendee journey design, roundtable prompts, networking formats, sponsor boundaries, content permissions, post-event reporting, measurement, debriefs, archives, future-city lessons, and repeatable event templates where your program, VIP, speaker, or session expertise is needed.
