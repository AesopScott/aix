## Offer Summary

Ron Bruno,

Mojo AI Summits would like to bring you in as the lead for curated executive attendance, VIP relationship development, executive hosting, and selected public-facing relationship moments for the company.

The current event model depends on a curated room of approximately 150 director-and-above leaders, with a strong executive mix across CEO, CFO, COO, CIO, CISO, CAIO/CTO, and related senior operating roles. Your role would be to help make that room credible, relationship-driven, and valuable to the leaders we invite.

The working compensation target is approximately $7,500 per event, paid monthly during the lead-up period.

For the Dallas event, the proposed payment structure is:

- $1,250 per month beginning after acceptance and continuing monthly through the event lead-up.
- If monthly lead-up payments do not total the full event fee by the event date, the remaining balance up to the agreed event fee will be paid within 10 business days after the event, assuming the responsibilities below have been substantially completed.
- Reasonable pre-approved event expenses, including approved travel, hosting, or meeting costs, will be reimbursed separately and are not included in the event fee.

This is intended as a practical event contractor/advisor arrangement. It does not create an employment relationship unless we later agree to that in a separate written agreement.

## Role

Your proposed role is Executive Relationship Director and VIP Host for Mojo AI Summit Dallas.

The role has five major outcomes:

1. Build and activate the curated director-and-above invitation strategy.
2. Recruit and help secure executives, VIPs, keynote candidates, anchor speakers, roundtable leaders, and advisory board participants.
3. Own the executive relationship layer before, during, and after the event.
4. Help make the event feel like a high-trust executive forum, not a generic vendor event.
5. Carry forward the best executive relationships into future Mojo AI Summits events, advisory boards, speaking roles, and private formats.

## Responsibilities

### Curated Attendance

You will own the curated invitation process for director-and-above leaders. This includes defining the ideal attendee profile, qualification rules, target company size criteria, and prioritization logic for the invite list.

You will build and manage the target invite list with other team members, launch individual invitation outreach via our marketing team, track responses with our Program Director, and help maintain relationship notes across confirmed attendees, declines, speaking prospects, roundtable leaders, executive attendees, and VIPs in the company CRM.

You will coordinate with marketing and sponsor outreach so executive invitees do not receive duplicate, confusing, or overly sales-oriented messages.

### Executive Recruitment and VIP Development

You will recruit executives for our advisory boards, roundtables, panels, fireside chats, private sessions, keynote consideration, and anchor speaking roles.

You will help define and confirm the VIP role list, including attendees who have speaking roles, roundtable leadership roles, board participation, or other strategic contributions.

Your role is to make sure the VIP experience stays organized, clear, and relationship-forward.

### Advisory Boards and VIP Groups

You will own or help lead the Sponsor Executive Board, Mojo AI Summits Cohort Advisory Board prospecting and invitations, and any Dallas Event VIP group if that group is used.

This includes building prospect lists, inviting members, scheduling board briefings as needed, planning the VIP dinner, deciding whether the Sponsor Executive Board dinner should happen after the event, and helping turn executive recommendations into event actions.

### Executive Experience and Public Presence

You will help define the executive networking moments, VIP and board dinner experience plans, and the public-facing role you will play at the event.

You will also plan and produce YouTube video interviews where useful for building executive credibility, sponsor confidence, and event momentum.

### On-Site Event Role

On event day, you will be responsible for identifying VIP and priority executive arrivals, greeting and hosting those guests, making high-value introductions, managing executive-facing requests, and helping keep the executive tone elevated.  You will also be an MC where logistics suits that outcome.

You will help protect the event from feeling overly transactional or sponsor-heavy. You will also handle executive relationship concerns and, if agreed, deliver closing remarks or a final executive sendoff.

### Post-Event Relationship Follow-Up

After the event, you will help send VIP and executive attendee thank-you notes, follow up individually with priority executives, capture relationship notes and next steps, compile executive feedback, and identify which executives should be invited into future events, advisory boards, speaking roles, or private formats.

You will also update executive invitation templates as needed and roll forward the executive relationship pipeline for future events.
