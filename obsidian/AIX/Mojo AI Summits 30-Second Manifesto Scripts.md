# Mojo AI Summits 30-Second Manifesto Scripts

Created: 2026-08-14

Durable summary: The current Mojo AI Summits manifesto set has been rebuilt into two 30-second direct-to-camera scripts: the homepage promise manifesto for Scott Schindler and the calendar clarity manifesto for Robert Haldeman. Both scripts preserve the no-hype, executive, no-sales-room positioning and avoid using "signal" for human contribution.

Canonical repo copy: `docs/marketing/mojo-ai-summits-30-second-manifesto-scripts.md`

## Scripts

### Homepage Promise Manifesto

Speaker: Scott Schindler, co-founder and CTO

```text
Somewhere along the way, executive events stopped serving executives.

They became content theaters. Sales funnels with better lighting. Rooms shaped less by what leaders need to know, and more by who paid to be near them.

Mojo AI Summits exists to change that.

We do not sell the room to the highest bidder. We do not put people on stage because they bought access.

We build rooms where leaders tell the truth, compare what is working, and leave sharper.

That is the standard.
```

### Calendar Clarity Manifesto

Speaker: Robert Haldeman, co-founder and CEO

```text
A bigger calendar does not mean a better event.

More programming does not mean more value. A bigger crowd does not mean a better conversation. And a packed schedule does not matter if no one leaves clearer.

Mojo AI Summits uses the calendar differently.

We are not trying to create noise. We are trying to create clarity.

Each room has a job: frame the decision, bring the right leaders together, compare what is working, and carry that insight forward into briefs, summits, and future rooms.
```
