# Dallas VIP Attendance Model

Created: 2026-07-28

## Working Decision

The first Dallas event can be modeled around 75 VIP registrations instead of 150 curated leaders, as long as VIP registrations count only the curated executive room. Sponsors, staff, and operational attendees are separate from the VIP count.

Speakers are VIPs. Roundtable leaders are VIPs. The VIP category should include any curated executive attendee with a meaningful contribution role, not only people with keynote or stage time.

## Attendance Assumption

A passive 75-registration model may produce about 62 attendees. A higher-attendance model is possible if every VIP has an active role and the reminder system is strong.

Working target:

- 75 VIP registrations
- 62 baseline attendee expectation
- 72 stretch attendee expectation with active roles and SMS reminders

## Program Shape

Breakouts should be treated as problem-solving rooms, not generic panels or passive sessions. Each room should focus on a real business problem and bring executives together to compare constraints, pressure-test ideas, and identify practical ways forward.

Every VIP should have an active role where possible. Roles can include speaker, roundtable leader, problem opener, peer respondent, synthesis lead, or executive contributor.

## Sponsor Model

Sponsor participation remains viable at 75 VIP registrations if the room quality is high and sponsor access is tied to problem-solving contribution rather than generic lead generation.

Sponsors that send an executive participant fit the standard participation model. Sponsors that send sales or marketing representatives should pay more or receive more restricted access, because commercial attendees increase room pressure and must meet a higher contribution standard.

## Open Reconciliation

The broader setup checklist still references the previous 150-leader model. If the 75-VIP model becomes the official plan, update event sizing, sponsor inventory, registration categories, room design, reminder operations, and revenue scenarios together.
