## Key Decision

Use `Independent Professional Guest` for a qualified external professional who was not on the original invite list but is approved to attend through application or ticket purchase.

This term should replace public-facing uses such as ticket buyer, paid attendee, external attendee, delegate, or generic attendee when referring to this access category.

## Notes

- The repository did not previously have a taxonomy or glossary file.
- Existing content still uses legacy terms such as attendee, sponsor, vendor, member, guest, and partner.
- Future copy cleanup should use `docs/taxonomy.md` as the source of truth, but bulk replacements should be reviewed because some legacy terms may be correct in operational, legal, finance, or internal contexts.
