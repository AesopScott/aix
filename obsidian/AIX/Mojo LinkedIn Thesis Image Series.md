# Mojo LinkedIn Thesis Image Series

Updated: 2026-08-21

Created a reusable LinkedIn image-series plan for Mojo AI Summits that uses editorial thought-leadership artwork with Mojo as the publisher.

Canonical repo artifact:

- `docs/marketing/mojo-linkedin-thesis-image-series.md`

Key decisions:

- Use an editorial image format: contemporary AI issue, executive consequence, strong thought-leadership line, and subtle Mojo event tie-in.
- Keep the visual style high-contrast and cinematic, using the Mojo palette from the brand guide: deep navy, electric blue, cyan, slate, and white.
- Tie the series to the existing 2026-2027 virtual event backbone.
- Prioritize executive stakes over webinar-style promotion.
- Do not name guests, companies, titles, or use portraits unless publication and likeness permissions are confirmed.
- Creative references: Fast Company, Wired, and Bloomberg Businessweek cover energy.
- Locked visual rules: small exact Mojo lockup top left, one cinematic executive scene, lots of negative space, restrained cyan, no icon rows, no fake dashboards, no boxed sections, no giant CTA bar, no fake speakers, no generic AI shield/calendar/people visuals, and no em dashes.

Seed issue hooks:

- Agent governance mismatch
- AI Act enforcement and transparency
- AI-enabled breach economics
- Generative AI lifecycle risk management
